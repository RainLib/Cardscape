# Collectible styles artwork

The workshops embed the original illustration plates created with ImageGen. The far environment, midground scenery, and foreground props have independent layer positions. The title lettering, light effects, and grayscale relief maps were authored after the artwork was registered.

For each pair of depth props, the top cutout becomes the midground scenery and the bottom cutout becomes an independently moving foreground element. The blade on Stormglass Oath was rebuilt along the gauntlet and guard axis after registering the grip correction.
