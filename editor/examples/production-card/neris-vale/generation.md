# Neris Vale production notes

[Neris Vale](neris-vale.cardworkshop) is an original sea-cartographer card assembled with the [Card Atelier skill](../../../skills/card-atelier/SKILL.md). The workshop contains the registered character master, hidden-paint candidate, editable split parts, masks, and both image wordmarks.

The frame aperture holds the torso and far arm. The hair, leading hand, cape, and sash can cross the marked edges. The hand and brass cuff share a rigid plane with a shallow offset from the sleeve, so they stay attached as the card turns. The sleeve and coat behind the split regions have painted support that can show during a tilt. The wordmark sits in front of the coat and behind the free end of the sash.

The built-in **Neris Vale** character preset drives the sash, loose hair, and trailing cape independently. The hair stays connected to the portrait and follows a tapered **Motion** mask. The sash and cape use fixed attachment points. The **Emission** and **Phase** masks move light around the engraved rose on the buckle and cuff. The cuff and the two letterforms use separate highlight sweeps. **Sea Breeze** is a 3.6-second idle action. **Show Both Faces** turns the card over to its compass-rose reverse.

The README captures come from the exported WebGL player. The full-card loop rotates the English version. The Chinese close-up holds the card angle fixed so the sash, engraving, and lettering can be inspected independently of the camera.

## Character master

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Original collectible-card character master on true transparent background, portrait 2:3. Paint one striking adult fantasy sea-cartographer named Neris Vale in a vivid high-end painted game illustration. Luminous silver-white flowing hair, teal and deep indigo tailored coat with narrow warm brass edging and sea-glass pauldrons, distinctive face and crystal blue gaze. Dramatic three-quarter nearly full-body lunging silhouette. The head and torso are centered near the upper-middle so they fit inside a card aperture. Show her own left arm on the viewer's right as the obvious closest surface: the fitted turquoise sleeve comes from her shoulder toward camera, then a large clearly defined solid brass cuff and one anatomically graceful open gloved hand reach far right; all fingers and cuff visible with clean outlines. Her far arm sits close to the waist, partly behind her. A wide deep blue cape trails behind her to viewer's left. One bright split teal silk ribbon curves separately from her belt down toward the lower-right quadrant, in front of a darker interior coat. Give that ribbon a distinct clean silhouette for separation and make it end above the bottom edge. Broad warm key light on cheek and cuff, a cool cyan rim light on hair and silk, richest contrast at the face. Crisp figure edges all around and spare clear alpha margins for both hands, hair, coat and ribbons. No background, card frame, lettering, platform, shield, weapons, watermark, ground shadow or floating props.
```

## Sea observatory

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Original painterly fantasy environment plate for a 2.5D card, portrait 2:3, no characters and no type. A ruined tidal observatory on dark coastal terraces, looking out through broken curved stonework over an illuminated blue-green sea at dusk. Deep inky ultramarine and smoky teal, warm gilded sundown at the far horizon, intricate streaks of reflected turquoise light in the lower quarter. Put the more defined stone shapes toward the sides of the frame and keep the upper center and middle relatively dark and uncluttered for a silver-haired human figure. Cinematic high contrast, blue depth haze, sharp layered wave texture, amber and sea-glass highlights with an atmospheric sense of depth. No glyphs, card frame, logo, people, sailboat, or watermark.
```
