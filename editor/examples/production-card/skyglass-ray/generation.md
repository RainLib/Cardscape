# Skyglass Ray artwork

The foreground ray and cloud terraces were generated separately with the built-in ImageGen tool. Both images are embedded in the editable workshop. The foreground retains its alpha, and the engraved back was drawn for this sample. [Open the workshop](skyglass-ray.cardworkshop)

## Foreground prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production-quality transparent foreground illustration for an original 2.5D collectible card. One elegant fantasy sky manta ray with a narrow tapered body, huge asymmetrically swept translucent fins, and one long slender ribbon tail. Three-quarter view, gliding from lower left toward upper right, seen gently from above. Spread the pointed fins across the canvas with an eye-catching readable silhouette. Keep every fin tip and the entire tail inside the image, with generous clean alpha margin. Pearl ivory belly and head, clear blue-green sea-glass fins with finely ridged caustics, small accents of warm brass and lavender. Crisp detailed painterly fantasy illustration under broad warm light. Give the translucent glass-like fin edges beautiful natural definition without a diffuse halo. True transparent background everywhere outside the creature, including between tail and fin. Isolated single figure. No card, lettering, prop, particle, frame, landscape, ground shadow, checkerboard, or watermark. Near square image.
```

## Environment prompt

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Create an original high-end fantasy trading-card background plate, just the environment, portrait 2:3 composition. A sea of cloud pools and floating blue-black rock terraces at blue hour, far above a quiet pale-amber sunrise. Cool indigo and cerulean in the upper two thirds, thin warm peach-gold sun rays around the horizon and a veil of deeper teal mist at the bottom. Make the large upper middle area a calm rich mid-dark blue sky, so a separately composited ivory translucent sky manta ray will silhouette clearly across it. Texture-rich, softly luminous painterly detail and strong aerial distance, with the most interesting cloud shapes lower down. No creature, people, diagram, game card border, brand, text, icons, or watermark.
```
